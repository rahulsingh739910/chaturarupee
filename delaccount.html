Delete Your Account

This guide explains how to delete your Chatura Rupee account from inside the app, what data is deleted, and how to ask us to delete your account if you can no longer sign in.

Effective 29 September 2026 India Chatura Rupee for Android
Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data yourself, inside the app, in a few taps — you do not need to write to us first. Deletion is permanent: your account is closed, your personal and KYC data and your loan records are deleted, and they cannot be restored.

If you still have a loan that is being repaid, your deletion request may not go through. Clear the outstanding amount first — see Before you start below.

If you can no longer sign in, you can still have your account deleted by writing to rahulsingh739910@gmail.com from the mobile number registered on the account.

The short version

  • Sign in, then go to Mine › Settings › Delete Account and confirm the dialog. That is the whole process.
  • Deleting your account is permanent — it cannot be undone or restored, so do it only if you are sure.
  • If you have a loan that is still being repaid, deletion may not succeed — settle the outstanding amount first.
  • As soon as you confirm, we sign you out, clear the data stored on your device, and return you to the sign-in screen.
  • You can also request deletion by email. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete the data you already sent us.

Before you start

To delete your account from inside the app you need to be able to sign in to it:

  • Sign in first — the option is only available to a signed-in user. If you cannot receive the verification code on your registered mobile number, use the email route in section 5.
  • No loan still being repaid — if you have a loan that is not fully repaid, or an instalment that is due, your deletion may not go through. Repay the outstanding amount first, then delete your account. If you are not sure whether anything is outstanding, email us before you start.
  • Be sure — once your account is deleted, your credit line, your KYC verification and your history are gone. If you want to use the app again later, you will have to register and complete KYC from the beginning.
  • Keep records you need — if you want a copy of your repayment history, save or screenshot it before you delete your account.
Deleting your account does not cancel a loan or the amount you still owe, and it does not remove an instalment that is due. If a repayment is outstanding when you tap Delete, the deletion may be refused and your account will remain registered. Repay first.
You can also stop further collection from your device at any time by uninstalling the app — but uninstalling does not delete information that has already been sent to us. To have that deleted, follow the steps below or email us.

How to delete your account in the app

Open the app while you are signed in, then follow these five steps. The whole thing takes less than a minute.

Mine› Settings› Delete Account› Delete
  1. Open the app and sign in Launch Chatura Rupee and sign in with your registered mobile number, if you are not already signed in. You will land on the Home screen.
  2. Tap the Mine tab In the bottom navigation bar, tap Mine — the third tab, on the right. Your profile screen opens, showing your registered mobile number.
  3. Tap Settings On the Mine screen, tap Settings, the last item in the list. The Settings screen opens. It also contains Privacy Policy and Logout.
  4. Tap Delete Account In Settings, tap the Delete Account item at the bottom of the list. A confirmation dialog appears. Nothing has been deleted yet.
  5. Tap Delete in the dialog The dialog asks: “Are you sure you want to delete your account and clear all your account data?” Tap Delete to confirm. To stop instead, tap Cancel — the dialog closes and nothing is deleted.
This is the point of no return. Once you tap Delete, your account and its data are deleted and cannot be recovered.

What happens after you confirm

When you tap Delete in the dialog, the app:

  • Sends the deletion request to our server, where your account and its personal data are deleted;
  • Signs you out and clears the data the app stored on your device;
  • Shows the message “All your account data has been cleared” and returns you to the sign-in screen.

Your account can no longer be used to sign in. If you want to use the app again, you can register with the same mobile number and complete KYC as a new user.

If a loan is still being repaid when you tap Delete, the app will still sign you out and clear the data on your device, but the deletion may not be completed on our side. You will notice this if you can still sign in with the same mobile number and your account details are still there. In that case, repay the outstanding amount and delete the account again, or contact us.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is deleted from our systems:

Account and login Your registered mobile number and the records used to sign you in.
Personal and financial profile Education, type of work, work experience, profession, job level, monthly income, living arrangement, marital status and number of children.
Identity (KYC) information Your full name, your PAN card number and the photograph of your PAN card.
Face verification The live photograph taken through the in-app camera during KYC.
References you chose The names and phone numbers of the contacts you selected.
Bank account details The bank card or account number used to disburse your loan and collect repayment.
Credit line, loans and repayments Your credit limit, your loan and repayment history, and any repayment reminders.
App usage and device data Your activity in the app and the device and risk-assessment data linked to your account.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your registered mobile number, or you cannot sign in for any other reason, you can still have your account deleted. Write to rahulsingh739910@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account, and include:

  • the mobile number registered on the account;
  • the full name you used for your KYC.

We will verify that the account is yours, then delete the account and its data. We aim to complete every verified deletion request within 30 days.

If a loan is still being repaid on the account, we may not be able to complete the deletion until the outstanding amount is settled — if that is the case, we will tell you and explain what is still due.

Please do not send us your PAN card photograph, your face photograph or your bank details by email — we do not need them to process a deletion request.
