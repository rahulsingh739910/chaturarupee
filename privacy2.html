Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the Chatura Rupee: Credit Line App for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Ram singh (collectively, the “Application”). Ram singh is referred to in this policy as the “Service Provider”, and also as “we”, “us” or “our”.

The Application provides a digital credit line to users in India. To do this, we need to collect and verify certain information about you. This policy tells you exactly what that information is.

By downloading, registering with, or using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Application.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you register and complete your KYC, you provide the following:

  • Account details — your mobile phone number and the verification code used to sign in.
  • Personal and financial profile — education level, type of work, years of work experience, profession, job level, monthly income, living arrangement, marital status, number of children, and religious belief.
  • Identity information — your full name, your PAN card number, and a photograph of your PAN card.
  • Face verification — a live photograph taken through the in-app camera, used to confirm that you are the person on the identity document.
  • References you choose — the name and phone number of up to two contacts that you select from your address book using the system contact picker. We do not read or upload your address book; only the contacts you deliberately pick are sent to us.
  • Bank account details — the bank card or account number used to disburse a loan and to collect repayment.

You may also contact us through the app (for example, through the Contact Us form), in which case we receive the content of your message and any contact details you choose to include.

Installed applications

To assess risk and detect fraudulent behaviour, the Application reads the list of applications installed on your device. For each application this includes the app name, package name, version name and code, first installation time, last update time, and whether it is a system application. This information is used as part of our automated risk assessment and is not used to identify you personally or to contact you.

Financial SMS messages

With your permission, the Application reads SMS messages stored on your device to support our credit assessment. We read and upload only messages that are related to your finances — for example, messages from banks and financial institutions about transactions, balances, loans, credit cards, payments, EMIs and other banking activity. We do not read, collect or upload your personal messages or any other non-financial SMS.

The financial SMS we collect are used for risk assessment and for analysing your financial status as part of our credit scoring and credit-limit modelling. All data we upload is encrypted before it leaves your device and while in transit to our server. Once the credit modelling for your application is complete, the original SMS data is deleted, and it is never sold, rented or shared with any third party for their own purposes.

Device and technical information

We automatically collect technical details about the device you use, including:

  • Device model, brand and device name, operating system version and API level;
  • Screen size, available storage and battery status;
  • Device and advertising identifiers, including the Android ID, your Google advertising ID, the Adjust identifier, and the Firebase application instance ID;
  • Network information, such as the connection type (Wi-Fi, 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G) and the Wi-Fi IP address;
  • The source from which you installed the Application (install referrer);
  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.

Location information

With your permission, the Application collects your device's approximate location (coarse location, derived from the network provider). We use it for location-based features, for fraud prevention, and to improve the Application and our services. We do not collect continuous background location tracking.

  • Geolocation services — location data may be used to provide location-based features or content.
  • Analytics and improvements — aggregated location data may help us understand usage patterns and improve performance.
  • Service providers — location data may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf, subject to this policy and applicable law.

How you use the Application

We collect information about your activity in the Application, including the pages and screens you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, the time spent in the Application, and your credit, loan and repayment records, including repayment reminders you create.

Permissions we request

The Application asks for the following permissions. Each is optional in the sense that you may refuse it — but the related feature, or your ability to complete KYC, may not work without it.

Camera To photograph your PAN card and to take the live photograph used for face verification.
Approximate location To record your general location at the time of your application, for fraud prevention and risk assessment.
Notifications To send you repayment reminders, due date alerts and other service messages (Android 13 and above).
Installed applications The Application queries the applications installed on your device for the risk assessment described in section 2. This does not require a runtime prompt on all versions of Android.
SMS To read and upload only financial SMS messages needed for credit risk assessment and financial status analysis, as described in section 2.
Network and Wi-Fi state To detect whether you are online and to deliver requests reliably.
We do not read your call history, and we do not read your address book as a whole. We read and upload only SMS messages related to your finances, as described in section 2. Contact details are only received for the specific contacts you choose to submit.

How we use your information

  • To verify your identity and assess your eligibility for a credit line;
  • To calculate your credit limit using our risk assessment model;
  • To disburse loans to you and to process your repayments;
  • To prevent, detect and investigate fraud, and to protect the security of our services;
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries and requests;
  • To send you important information and required notices about your account, and — where permitted by law — marketing communications;
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, including record-keeping requirements;
  • To understand how the Application is used and to improve its performance and features.

Where we rely on your consent to process your information, you may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

How your data is stored and protected

When you use the Application, the information you submit — including your KYC profile, your identity documents, your face photograph and the financial SMS data described in section 2 — is encrypted and uploaded to our own server at https://c5yaar.com. Request content is encrypted with AES before it leaves your device, and all communication between the Application and our server is carried over an encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connection.

Your data is used to calculate your credit limit and to provide the services described in this policy. It is not sold, rented, or traded, and it is not shared with third parties for their own purposes. Access to personal data inside our organisation is limited to staff and systems that need it to perform the risk assessment, verification and servicing of your account.

Data you submit for your assessment is processed to produce a risk score. Raw documents and data are not kept longer than necessary for the purposes described in this policy, our legal obligations, and our records requirements under section 10.
